# AFD-0 Open Problems

Open problems generated by AFD-0. These are *new* problems that arise from formalizing the Layer-2 abstract dynamics; they should be added to the AFD section of `theorem_status.md`'s Open Problems Catalog if/when AFD-0 promotes to canonical.

Severity legend: H (high — blocks Layer-2 results), M (medium — refines them), L (low — extensions).

## OP-AFD-001 — Continuity / Stability of Topology Signature at Vineyard Set

**Severity.** M.

**Statement.** AFD-D11 (TopSig τ) is bottleneck-Lipschitz by CSEH 2007. However on the vineyard set V ⊂ Σ_m (codim-1 semi-algebraic where bar-order changes), the *sorted* bar-vector is discontinuous. Quantify the residual continuity of τ near V in a form suitable for Var_D-style integration along paths γ that cross V.

**Goal.** Either (a) prove τ is sufficiently regular off V to make AFD-D9 / AFD-T2 (Inside, Persist components) fully rigorous in all required modes, or (b) supply an ε-smoothing operator τ_ε such that τ_ε is Lipschitz on all of Σ_m with τ_ε → τ as ε → 0 (uniformly off V).

**Relates to.** AFD-T2, `working/E/soft_K_definition.md` §2.3.

## OP-AFD-002 — K_act Stratification Regularity

**Severity.** M.

**Statement.** S_K = {u : K_act(u) = K} is defined as a set-theoretic level set. Determine the regularity class of S_K:

- Is S_K a Whitney-stratified subset of Σ_m?
- Is each S_K a semi-algebraic set?
- Is the boundary ∂S_K = closure(S_K) \ S_K equal to the vineyard set V (or its K-relevant subset)?
- Under what additional hypotheses (definability, transversality of E to V, persistence-regularity) does S_K become a smooth submanifold of T Σ_m of computable codimension?

**Goal.** A precise regularity statement that justifies calling S_K a "stratum" in the differential-topology sense, OR a clean statement that S_K is only a set-theoretic decomposition and should not be called a stratum.

**Relates to.** AFD-D12 warning, AFD-T3, `canonical.md` §3.11 D-ST-3.

## OP-AFD-003 — Existence of Minimizing Transition Paths

**Severity.** H.

**Statement.** Is the infimum in AFD-D7 (`C_AFD(F_i, F_j) = inf_γ J_AFD(γ; F_i)`) attained by some admissible path γ_* ∈ Adm(F_i, F_j)?

In the minimal version (λ_D = λ_K = 0) this is: does there exist a *minimizing barrier path* — a continuous γ ∈ Adm(F_i, F_j) achieving `max_s (E(γ(s)) − E_F_i) = Bar(F_i, F_j)`?

**Goal.** Either prove attainment (Arzelà-Ascoli + lower semicontinuity of sup along reparametrization-invariant rectifiable curves of bounded length), or supply an explicit counterexample on a small graph.

**Relates to.** AFD-T5, FW instanton existence, OP-AFD-005.

## OP-AFD-004 — Positive Merge Barrier (Analytic Lower Bound)

**Severity.** H → **PARTIALLY RESOLVED (Cat B, 2026-05-12).**

**Statement.** Prove an analytic lower bound

> `C_K(K, K−1) ≥ c(β, n, G) > 0` for all K ≥ 2,

where c is an explicit positive function of the inverse-temperature analogue β, the graph size n, and the graph G.

**Resolution (2026-05-12, Cat B).** See `op_afd_004_proof.md` for full argument.

- **Strategy A (qualitative, Cat B):** Basin-exit argument. u*_K is a strict local minimizer (T8-Core Cat A). u*_{K-1} lies outside basin B(F_K) (different critical point). Every admissible merging path must exit B(F_K). Exit costs ≥ Δ_min(F_K) > 0. Therefore Bar(F_K, F_{K-1}) ≥ Δ_min(F_K) > 0. H-MORSE NOT used.

- **Strategy B (quantitative, Cat B conditional):** T-Persist-1(b) gives Δ_core ≥ 0.0441β. K=2 joint basin depth ≥ 0.0221β under H1-H4+WS+SR conditions. Therefore **c_low(β) = 0.0221β** is an explicit lower bound. Validated: exp38 gives refined barrier ~23.5 at β=50; c_low = 1.1 (factor ~21 conservative).

**Remaining sub-problems.**

- **OP-AFD-004a** (L3): Tight exponent β^0.89 (or β^1.2) analytic derivation. Requires H-MORSE-Saddle + Modica neck geometry.
- **OP-AFD-004b** (L2): General K without WS/SR conditions.
- **OP-AFD-004c** (L2+): Near-bifurcation (μ→0) lower bound.

**AFD-T7 status:** Lemma Candidate (C_K > 0) → **Cat B Proposition** (conditional on H1-H4+WS+SR).

**Relates to.** AFD-T7, OP-0005 K-Selection. Proof in `op_afd_004_proof.md`.

## OP-AFD-005 — FW-to-SCC Refinement Theorem (Full EK Compatibility)

**Severity.** H.

**Statement.** Prove rigorously that for the reflected gradient Langevin SDE on the convex polytope Σ_m (T-PF-A1-SDE / AR / GI / PE all Cat A), the FW large-deviation rate function `I_T(γ)` has quasipotential `V(u_F_i^*, u_F_j^*) = Bar(F_i, F_j)`, and that under H-MORSE-Local + H-MORSE-Saddle the mean first-passage time satisfies

> `T_* log E[τ_{i → j}] → Bar(F_i, F_j)` as T_* → 0.

**Components needed.**

- (a) FW theory adapted to reflected Langevin on convex polytopes with corners.
- (b) Quasipotential = energy-barrier identity for gradient SDEs (standard) + reflected-boundary correction.
- (c) Bovier-Eckhoff-Gayrard EK formula extended to convex polytopes (literature gap noted in `CV114_H_MORSE_PACKAGEII/06_packageII_dependency_map.md` §2.5).
- (d) Identification of the saddle-path geometry with the AFD barrier minimizer (when H-MORSE holds).

**Relates to.** AFD-T8, CV-1.14, Package II.

## OP-AFD-008 — Topology-Aware K-Jump Cost

**Severity.** L.

**Statement.** AFD-D10 sets `J_K(γ) = TV(K_act ∘ γ)`. A finer cost would weight K-jumps by their topological type (birth vs merge vs split), the persistence-lifetime of the affected bars, and the geometric distance between merging formations.

**Goal.** Define a refined `J_K^{topo}(γ)` using the vineyard structure and the persistence-lifetime weights, and prove its Lipschitz / lower-semicontinuity properties.

**Relates to.** OP-AFD-001, σ-rich / Φ-rich framework (OP-0008, Commitment 18 candidate).

## OP-AFD-010 — Finiteness of V_form

**Severity.** M.

**Statement.** For generic SCC parameters (β, α, λ_cl, λ_sep, λ_bd, λ_tr, m, G), is V_form finite?

For the (β, α, ...)-Morse case the local-minimizer set of E on Σ_m is generically finite (standard Morse theory on a compact manifold). Without H-MORSE the answer is unclear: degenerate critical manifolds (Goldstone families) generate uncountable V_form unless equivalence classes (AFD-D4) are used.

**Goal.** A clean statement of when V_form is finite, and a recommendation for when to switch to V_form / ~ (equivalence classes) or to V_form / Aut(G).

**Relates to.** AFD-D4, AFD-T10, OP-0008, OP-AFD-009.
