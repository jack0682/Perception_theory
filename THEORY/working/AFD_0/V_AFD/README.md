# V-AFD — Vector Abstract Formation Dynamics

This subfolder contains the **V-AFD** working draft: a vector-projection refinement of AFD-0 in which SCC formation dynamics is studied through diagnostic vectors and structural coordinates rather than raw cohesion fields.

## What this is

V-AFD is an **abstraction**, not a replacement. It takes the AFD-0 formation-state tuple `F = (u_F^*, B_F, d_F, K_F, τ_F, E_F)` (AFD-D3) and promotes the tuple itself to the primary object of study. The map

> `π_Z : Σ_m → 𝒵 := [0,1]⁴ × {1,…,K_field} × ℝ × PD`,
> `π_Z(u) = (D(u), K_act(u), E(u), τ(u))`

is the central technical device. V-AFD is the language of vector trajectories `z_γ(s) = Z(γ(s))`, Pareto preorders on diagnostic vectors, vector transition costs, and the quotient graph `G_V = G_form / ~_Z`.

## What this is not

- Not a replacement for AFD-0 (parent doc at `../abstract_formation_dynamics.md`).
- Not a replacement for SCC field dynamics on `Σ_m`.
- Not a proof of H-MORSE or Eyring-Kramers prefactors (Layer 3, deferred).
- Not a Markov-chain model of formation dynamics (open: OP-VAFD-003).
- Not a metric (`C_V` is asymmetric and may violate triangle).

## Slogan to remember

> AFD-0 builds the state graph `G_form`.
> V-AFD builds the vector quotient `π_Z : G_form → G_V` and tracks dynamics inside the diagnostic vector image.
> EK / H-MORSE remains a Layer-3 refinement of selected edges, unchanged by V-AFD.
