# V-AFD Open Problems

Open problems generated by the V-AFD vector-projection reformulation. These are *new* problems that arise from making AFD-0's vector-implicit content explicit. They should be cross-referenced with the existing OP-AFD-001..010 in `../afd_open_problems.md`.

Severity legend: H (high — blocks V-AFD theorems), M (medium — refines), L (low — extension).

---

## OP-VAFD-001 — Continuity / Lipschitz Status of D Under Vineyard Crossings

**Severity.** M.

**Statement.** AFD-T2 (Cat A) gives D Lipschitz on Σ_m globally. However, the Inside component depends on persistence-diagram features (CSEH 2007 bottleneck-Lipschitz with d_B, but sorted-bar reordering happens at the vineyard set V). Quantify the Lipschitz constants of each component (Bind, Sep, Inside, Persist) per region of Σ_m, especially in tubular neighborhoods of V.

**Relates to.** OP-AFD-001 (TopSig continuity at V), AFD-T2, V-AFD-T5.

**Goal.** Per-component Lipschitz constants explicit, or proof that the global Lipschitz constant suffices for the V-AFD trajectory-level BV bounds.

## OP-VAFD-002 — Persist Coordinate for Static vs Temporal States

**Severity.** M.

**Statement.** V-AFD-D1 uses the static placeholder `Persist(u) = 1`. The pairwise / window forms (V-AFD-D1' / D1'') are well-defined under T-Temporal-Identity Cat A, but require additional inputs (transition operator M, time window). When V-AFD analyzes a *temporal* SCC trajectory `{u_t}`, what is the right vector state? Does each `t` carry a vector with pairwise Persist relative to a fixed reference? Or does V-AFD operate on *pair-vectors* `(Z(u_t), Z(u_s))`?

**Goal.** A precise specification of V-AFD's temporal-extension protocol: when to use static, pairwise, or window forms, and the corresponding definitions of vector trajectory.

**Relates to.** T-Temporal-Identity (canonical CV-1.13 Cat A), V-AFD-D1.

## OP-VAFD-003 — Markovianity / Lumpability of Vector Dynamics

**Severity.** H.

**Statement.** Is the process `t \mapsto Z(u(t)) ∈ 𝒵` induced by SCC dynamics (deterministic gradient flow at zero noise, or reflected Langevin at finite noise) Markov? If not Markov, is there a Lumpable structure (Kemeny-Snell 1976) under which V-AFD predicts long-run behavior?

**Sub-questions:**
- (a) Deterministic case: `Z` alone is *not* sufficient for Markovianity (V-AFD-T9 vector-degeneracy). Is `Z_+ = (Z, basin\_label)` sufficient?
- (b) Stochastic case (T_* > 0): basin label fluctuates. Lumpability requires equivalence of transition probabilities; the relevant question is whether `π_Z`-orbits of the Markov field process project to a Markov chain on `V_Z`.
- (c) Approximate Markovianity: is `Z` an *approximate* Markov coordinate in some asymptotic regime (T_* → 0, large n)?

**Relates to.** OP-AFD-006 (Diagnostic Coarse-Graining and Markov / Lumpability), V-AFD-T12.

**Goal.** A clean statement of when V-AFD provides a Markov chain on `V_Z`, with characterizable hypotheses.

## OP-VAFD-004 — Injectivity Loss of Z Projection (Characterization)

**Severity.** M.

**Statement.** V-AFD-T9 establishes that `π_Z : V_\mathrm{form} \to V_Z` is non-injective in general, with three example sources: (i) Aut(G)-symmetry, (ii) Goldstone families, (iii) topological coincidence. Quantify *when* this loss matters dynamically:

- For Layer-2 transition-ordering questions: does vector-degeneracy ever change the answer? (Conjecture: no, because degenerate fibers are dynamically equivalent under SCC operators.)
- For barrier-cost computation: does the quotient cost `w_Z` (V-AFD-D11 inf-aggregation) lose load-bearing information? (Conjecture: no for Layer-2 ordering; yes for *which specific* edge realizes the cost.)
- For Layer-3 stochastic refinement: vector-degenerate fibers must be quotiented or basin-labeled. (Required: V-AFD does not currently specify Layer-3 vector-degeneracy handling.)

**Goal.** Theorem of form "V-AFD-T9 information loss does/does not affect V-AFD-T6 cost / V-AFD-T11 graph / V-AFD-T8 EK rate." Currently: V-AFD-T9 is established but its dynamical significance is not characterized.

**Relates to.** V-AFD-T9, V-AFD-D12, OP-AFD-009 (Conley-index AFD-1).

## OP-VAFD-005 — Topology Signature Stability Beyond Lipschitz

**Severity.** M.

**Statement.** τ is Lipschitz in the bottleneck metric `d_B` (CSEH 2007). However, the *sorted-bar vector* representation of τ is discontinuous at V. V-AFD-T4 treats τ as a càdlàg coordinate; V-AFD-T5 uses `L_τ = 1` in the bottleneck metric. Are there V-AFD applications that require **stronger** stability (e.g. Lipschitz in Wasserstein-2 distance, or smooth in some persistence-derivative metric)?

**Goal.** Characterize the regularity of τ in alternative metrics on PD (W_2, sliced-Wasserstein, persistence landscapes) and identify which V-AFD theorems would generalize.

**Relates to.** OP-AFD-001, V-AFD-D2, V-AFD-T5.

## OP-VAFD-006 — Vector Transition Cost Attainment in Enriched Form

**Severity.** M.

**Statement.** V-AFD-T6' establishes attainment of `C_V` minimal version under Claim B.3. For the **enriched version** with `λ_τ > 0, λ_L > 0`: does the infimum attain?

**Sub-questions:**
- (a) `λ_τ > 0`: lower-semicontinuity of `Var_τ(γ)` under uniform convergence. CSEH 2007 gives bottleneck-LSC; combined with rectifiability bounds yields attainment.
- (b) `λ_L > 0`: `Len(γ)` is LSC under uniform convergence (standard length-LSC); attainment follows.

**Conjecture.** Attainment extends to the full enriched form, Cat A modulo Claim B.3.

**Goal.** Prove the conjecture explicitly. Compare to OP-AFD-003c-K_act (the K_act variant).

**Relates to.** V-AFD-T6', T-OP-AFD-003-C (`logs/daily/2026-05-12/02_development.md`), Claim B.3 (`02b_L3_tightening_and_op003c.md`).

## OP-VAFD-007 — K-Jump Regularity for Non-Transversal Paths

**Severity.** L.

**Statement.** V-AFD-T10 establishes that for transversal rectifiable γ, K_act∘γ is piecewise constant with finitely many jumps. For non-transversal γ (γ dwells on V on positive-measure subset of `[0, 1]`), the jump structure may degenerate.

**Goal.** Either (a) prove transversality is generic in Adm(F_i, F_j) under sup-norm (Baire-genericity), or (b) extend V-AFD to handle non-transversal paths via generalized BV.

**Relates to.** OP-AFD-003c-K_act (vineyard transversality), V-AFD-T10.

## OP-VAFD-008 — Empirical Estimation of Vector Dynamics

**Severity.** L.

**Statement.** Given a numerical SCC simulation (e.g. `CODE/experiments/exp60_*.py`), can V-AFD predict the vector trajectory `z(t)` with quantitative accuracy?

**Sub-questions:**
- (a) Computational complexity of `Z(u)` per timestep (relevant for `K_act`, `τ` which require persistence computation).
- (b) Accuracy of empirical Pareto preorder relative to ground-truth Pareto preorder.
- (c) Vector-cost estimation: given a numerical path γ from `find_formation` or NEB, what is `C_V(γ)`?

**Goal.** Reproducible empirical estimation protocol; baseline numerical V-AFD on canonical 15×15 grid.

**Relates to.** `CODE/experiments/`, V-AFD-D6.

## OP-VAFD-010 — Relation to OP-AFD-004 (Merge Barrier)

**Severity.** M.

**Statement.** OP-AFD-004 Cat B (resolved 2026-05-12) gives `C_K(K, K-1) ≥ 0.0221β > 0`. In V-AFD language, this is a lower bound on the K-jump component of `C_V` for merge transitions.

**V-AFD reformulation:** for any vector transition `Z_i ⇒ Z_j` with K decreasing (i.e. `K_{F_i} > K_{F_j}`), the vector cost satisfies

$$C_V(F_i, F_j) \geq \lambda_E \cdot 0.0221\beta + \lambda_K \cdot |K_{F_i} - K_{F_j}|$$

(modulo H1-H4+WS+SR conditions of OP-AFD-004 Strategy B).

**Goal.** Confirm the V-AFD lower bound; prove it as a V-AFD-T-MergeLB candidate.

**Relates to.** OP-AFD-004 Cat B, AFD-T7 Cat B, V-AFD-D6 K-jump component.
