# Vector Abstract Formation Dynamics (V-AFD)

A coarse-grained, vector-projection refinement of AFD-0 in which formation dynamics is studied through diagnostic vectors and structural coordinates rather than raw cohesion fields.

---

## 0. Executive Summary

**V-AFD** = Vector Abstract Formation Dynamics.

V-AFD takes the AFD-0 formation-state tuple `(u_F^*, B_F, d_F, K_F, τ_F, E_F)` (per AFD-D3) and **promotes the tuple itself to the primary object of study**, in contrast to AFD-0's view of formation states as nodes in `G_form` whose dynamics is implicitly governed by field-level transitions.

Concretely:

> **V-AFD studies, for each field `u ∈ Σ_m`, the vector**
> $$Z(u) = (D(u),\ K_\mathrm{act}(u),\ E(u),\ \tau(u))$$
> **and the induced vector trajectories `z_γ(s) = Z(γ(s))` along field paths γ.**

V-AFD is a **projection / coarse-graining** of SCC field dynamics. The projection
$$\pi_Z : \Sigma_m \;\to\; \mathcal{Z} = [0,1]^4 \times \{1,\dots,K_\mathrm{field}\} \times \mathbb{R} \times \mathrm{PD}$$
loses information by design. The mathematical content of V-AFD is exactly the characterization of *what* is lost, *when* the lost information matters, and *what additional coordinates* can be appended to `Z` to recover specific reasoning chains.

**Headline framing (slogan).**

> AFD-0 builds the state graph `G_form`.
> V-AFD builds the *vector quotient* `π_Z : G_form → G_V` and tracks dynamics inside the diagnostic vector image.
> EK / H-MORSE remains a Layer-3 refinement of selected edges, unchanged by V-AFD.

**Compatibility position.** V-AFD does **not** modify any canonical claim, any AFD-0 definition, any AFD-0 theorem statement, or any open problem in `afd_open_problems.md`. V-AFD adds a new layer of vector-language definitions and propositions on top of AFD-0. All AFD-T1..T10 results pass through V-AFD unchanged. V-AFD adds:

- V-AFD-D1..D12 (definitions in vector language)
- V-AFD-T1..T12 (theorem candidates, mostly Proposition/Theorem with explicit honest status)
- OP-VAFD-001..010 (new open problems generated by the vector formulation)

The remainder of this document is the detailed specification.

## 1. Motivation: why a vector method now

### 1.1 The two ways of looking at AFD-0

`abstract_formation_dynamics.md` §5–§7 specifies a formation state as a tuple `F = (u_F^*, B_F, d_F, K_F, τ_F, E_F)` and the dynamics between formation states as transitions in the directed graph `G_form`. Two reading orders are possible:

- **(A) Graph-first reading.** Formation states are nodes; the central objects are edges and edge weights (`C_AFD`); the central question is "what is the transition cost between two named states?" This is the explicit AFD-0 reading.
- **(B) Vector-first reading.** Formation states are *points in a structured vector space* `\mathcal{Z}`; the central object is the vector image of field trajectories; the central question is "how does a field's coarse-grained signature evolve under the SCC dynamics?" This is the V-AFD reading.

Both readings are mathematically compatible. The AFD-0 graph `G_form` is recovered as the image of `V_form` under `π_Z`, equipped with the inf-cost edge weight inherited from `C_AFD`. The vector reading **adds** the ability to discuss:

- continuous-time variation of D along a single γ (not just γ's endpoints),
- partial-order (Pareto) comparison of formation quality,
- K-jump events as discrete co-events alongside continuous diagnostic evolution,
- topology-signature drift between K-jumps,
- multi-criteria stability rather than scalar `ExitCost`.

These are all features that AFD-0 *implies* but does not *foreground*. V-AFD foregrounds them.

### 1.2 Why not exact Eyring-Kramers as foundation

The CV-1.14 / Package II programme aims at exact EK rates with prefactors. As `afd_hmorse_reclassification.md` documents, this requires:

- H-MORSE-Local (Cat B target, not Cat A),
- H-MORSE-Saddle (not yet registered),
- FW quasipotential identification (OP-AFD-005),
- reflected-Langevin EK adaptation (literature gap),
- T_* → 0 small-noise axiom (OP-0021).

None of these are needed to *order* transitions, to *compare* formation quality, or to *describe* how diagnostic vectors change along field paths. V-AFD takes the same architectural decision as AFD-0: **separate transition order from transition rate**. EK refines vector-cost ordering at Layer 3 when it applies; V-AFD provides the Layer-2 cost ordering itself, free of Hessian-determinant inputs.

### 1.3 What V-AFD adds beyond AFD-0

V-AFD is genuinely new relative to AFD-0 in:

(i) **Vector trajectory language.** `z_γ : [0,1] → \mathcal{Z}` is a well-defined map for each admissible field γ. AFD-0 implicitly uses `γ(0)` and `γ(1)` as basin endpoints and `max_s E(γ(s))` for the barrier; V-AFD tracks all coordinates of `Z` along the entire path.

(ii) **Pareto preorder on D.** AFD-T6 gives a *scalar* preorder via `ExitCost`. V-AFD-T2 gives a componentwise preorder on the diagnostic vector, which is finer (more incomparable pairs) but matches the SCC ontology more closely (diagnostics are independently meaningful, not summed).

(iii) **Hybrid / piecewise structure.** D is continuous (Lipschitz, AFD-T2); K_act is integer-valued; τ is Lipschitz in the bottleneck metric but the sorted-bar vector is discontinuous at V. V-AFD makes this hybrid structure explicit and assigns it the language of piecewise / càdlàg trajectories.

(iv) **Information-loss tracking.** Two basins with the same `(D, K_act, E, τ)` are vector-indistinguishable but dynamically distinct. V-AFD-T9 makes this loss explicit and frames it as an open problem to characterize *when* it matters (OP-VAFD-004).

(v) **Vector cost functional `C_V`.** A new cost mixing energy barrier, diagnostic variation, K-jump count, topology variation, and (optionally) path length / quality loss. The minimal version `C_V = Bar` reduces to AFD-D7 minimal version (`λ_D = λ_K = 0`); the enriched version adds explicit Pareto-weighted terms.

(vi) **Compatibility-by-projection.** V-AFD-T11 states that vector-graph dynamics is a *quotient* of formation-graph dynamics, with explicit identification of edge collapse and cost aggregation.

### 1.4 What V-AFD does not add

V-AFD does *not* claim:

- vector dynamics is Markovian (OP-VAFD-003 — open),
- vector projection is injective (V-AFD-T9 — explicitly non-injective),
- exact EK rates (Layer 3, unchanged),
- scalar diagnostic quality `Q` is canonical (only design-option, V-AFD-D9),
- Persist is a single-state coordinate (it is intrinsically temporal/pairwise — OP-VAFD-002),
- topology signature is smooth (it is Lipschitz in d_B but discontinuous in sorted-bar vector — OP-VAFD-005),
- K_act is continuous (it jumps at V — V-AFD-T10 honest acknowledgment).

### 1.5 Relation to the GPT-5 meta-research suggestion

The GPT-5 meta-research report (delivered earlier in this session) suggested:

> "Diagnostic vector dynamics" as one of five candidate abstractions (the others being Basin Graph Dynamics, K-Stratum Dynamics, Category/Functor Dynamics, Order-Theoretic Dynamics).

V-AFD selects "Diagnostic Vector Dynamics" as the central method and integrates the others as sub-aspects:

- **Basin Graph Dynamics** → V-AFD-T11 (vector graph as projection of formation graph).
- **K-Stratum Dynamics** → V-AFD-D10 (K-coordinate, K-jump events, K-stratum vector image).
- **Category/Functor Dynamics** → V-AFD-T11 + OP-VAFD-009 (deferred categorification).
- **Order-Theoretic Dynamics** → V-AFD-T2 (Pareto preorder), V-AFD-T3 (Lyapunov candidate).

So V-AFD is the *central* abstraction with the other four as sub-coordinates or follow-ups.

## 2. Relation to AFD-0 field dynamics

### 2.1 Field dynamics (AFD-0 input)

The SCC field dynamics is, in the deterministic AFD-D2 default:

$$\dot u = -P_T \nabla E(u),\qquad u(0) \in \Sigma_m,$$

with `P_T` the orthogonal projection onto the tangent space `T \Sigma_m`. T14 (Łojasiewicz Cat A) gives convergence to a critical point. AFD-D2 partitions `Σ_m` (modulo basin boundaries of measure zero) into deterministic basins.

V-AFD does **not** redefine this dynamics. V-AFD studies its *image* under `Z`:

### 2.2 The vector projection

**Vector image of the field state space:**

$$\mathcal{Z} \;:=\; [0,1]^4 \;\times\; \{1, \dots, K_\mathrm{field}\} \;\times\; \mathbb{R} \;\times\; \mathrm{PD},$$

where `\mathrm{PD}` is the space of finite persistence diagrams of `H_0` for superlevel-set filtrations on `Σ_m`, equipped with the bottleneck metric `d_B`.

**The projection map:**

$$\pi_Z : \Sigma_m \to \mathcal{Z},\qquad \pi_Z(u) \;:=\; \bigl(\,D(u),\;K_\mathrm{act}(u),\;E(u),\;\tau(u)\,\bigr).$$

`Z(u) = \pi_Z(u)` is the vector state of `u`.

**Image of dynamics:** For each field trajectory `t \mapsto u(t)` solving `\dot u = -P_T \nabla E`, the *vector trajectory* is

$$z(t) \;:=\; Z(u(t)) \;\in\; \mathcal{Z}.$$

V-AFD studies these vector trajectories.

### 2.3 The fundamental asymmetry

The map `\pi_Z` is **not invertible**. Multiple fields can share the same vector state (V-AFD-T9). Conversely, `Z` is defined on every `u ∈ Σ_m`, so every field has a *unique* image. The asymmetry:

- field → vector: well-defined, deterministic,
- vector → field: multi-valued, undefined uniquely.

This means: V-AFD can **predict** vector observables from field trajectories, but it cannot in general **reconstruct** the field from the vector. Whether reconstruction matters depends on the question — for transition existence (Layer 2) it usually does not; for exact rate (Layer 3) it does.

### 2.4 Why "Persist" is the awkward coordinate

The other three diagnostic coordinates (`Bind`, `Sep`, `Inside`) are scalar functions of a single field state `u`. `Persist` per `canonical.md` §7.2 is fundamentally **temporal**: it answers "how stable was the formation across a time window or transport pair?" Single-state `Persist` is a *placeholder* unless we explicitly specify:

- (Static placeholder) `Persist(u) := 1` (default for single-state V-AFD).
- (Pairwise) `Persist(u, v, M) :=` overlap of cores after applying transition operator `M`.
- (Window) `Persist({u_t : t ∈ [t_0, t_1]}) :=` core-overlap fraction over the window.

V-AFD adopts the **static placeholder** for the single-state vector `Z(u)` and reserves the temporal/pairwise variants for trajectory-level analysis. This decision is recorded in V-AFD-D1 and OP-VAFD-002.

## 3. Definitions

The definitions follow the AFD-0 numbering convention: `V-AFD-D1..D12`. Each definition is paired with the corresponding AFD-0 definition where one exists.

### V-AFD-D1 — Diagnostic Vector

**Static form.** For `u ∈ Σ_m`:

$$D(u) \;:=\; \bigl(\,\mathrm{Bind}(u),\; \mathrm{Sep}(u),\; \mathrm{Inside}(u),\; \mathrm{Persist}(u)\,\bigr) \;\in\; [0,1]^4.$$

The first three components are defined on a single field state via `canonical.md` §7.2:

- `Bind(u)`: cohesion-fraction inside the core (energy-bridge via `A3` closure, Cat A).
- `Sep(u)`: 1 − `E_sep`/m (Predicate-Energy Bridge, Cat A).
- `Inside(u)`: superlevel-set core mass fraction (via QM3 / persistence, Cat A modulo OP-AFD-001 vineyard).

The fourth component is treated as a **static placeholder**:

$$\mathrm{Persist}(u) \;:=\; 1 \quad \text{(default convention)}.$$

This default convention says: a single field state has no past to persist *from* and no future to persist *to*; the static value is a unit placeholder so that `D` is fully defined on `Σ_m`. The substantive temporal variants are V-AFD-D1' and V-AFD-D1''.

**Temporal pairwise form.** For two field states `u, v ∈ Σ_m` and a transition operator `M_{t→s}` (canonical §3, Cat A):

$$\mathrm{Persist}_{\mathrm{pair}}(u, v, M) \;:=\; \frac{|\mathrm{core}(u) \cap M^{-1}(\mathrm{core}(v))|}{|\mathrm{core}(u)|},$$

where `\mathrm{core}(u) := \{x : u_x \geq \theta_\mathrm{core}\}` (threshold per canonical §7.2). Bounded in [0, 1]. This is the temporal-pair diagnostic vector used by T-Temporal-Identity (canonical CV-1.13 Cat A).

**Window form.** For a time window `[t_0, t_1]` and a trajectory `\{u_t\}`:

$$\mathrm{Persist}_W(\{u_t\}) \;:=\; \inf_{t \in [t_0, t_1]} \mathrm{Persist}_{\mathrm{pair}}(u_{t_0}, u_t, M_{t_0 \to t}).$$

The minimum captures "worst-case persistence" across the window.

**Convention for V-AFD-D2..D12.** Unless explicitly stated, V-AFD definitions use the static form `D(u)` with `Persist(u) = 1`. The pairwise / window forms are flagged when used.

### V-AFD-D2 — Enriched Vector State

For `u ∈ Σ_m`:

$$Z(u) \;:=\; \bigl(\,D(u),\; K_\mathrm{act}(u),\; E(u),\; \tau(u)\,\bigr) \;\in\; \mathcal{Z}.$$

Components:

- `D(u) ∈ [0,1]^4` (V-AFD-D1).
- `K_\mathrm{act}(u) ∈ \{1, \dots, K_\mathrm{field}\}` (Commitment 16, Cat A).
- `E(u) ∈ \mathbb{R}` (canonical SCC energy on Σ_m).
- `\tau(u) ∈ \mathrm{PD}` (H_0 persistence diagram of the superlevel filtration of u, equipped with bottleneck distance `d_B`; CSEH 2007 Cat A).

The vector state space is `\mathcal{Z} = [0,1]^4 \times \{1, \dots, K_\mathrm{field}\} \times \mathbb{R} \times \mathrm{PD}`. The natural metric is the *product metric*

$$d_\mathcal{Z}(z, z') \;:=\; \|D - D'\|_2 \;+\; \mathbf{1}[K \neq K'] \;+\; |E - E'| \;+\; d_B(\tau, \tau'),$$

with optional weights. (The K-coordinate uses indicator distance because K is integer-valued; alternatives use `|K - K'|` if K is treated as integer-valued real.)

**Optional extension (richer vector).** For some applications:

$$Z_+(u) \;:=\; \bigl(\,D(u),\; K_\mathrm{act}(u),\; E(u),\; \tau(u),\; \mathrm{basin\_label}(u),\; \|\nabla E(u)\|_2\,\bigr).$$

The basin-label coordinate is the basin attractor `F` such that gradient flow from `u` converges to `u_F^*` (per AFD-D2). The gradient-norm coordinate is the local stationarity indicator. These are useful for V-AFD-T11 / V-AFD-T12 (Markovianity) but introduce non-Lipschitz dependencies. V-AFD baseline uses `Z`, not `Z_+`.

**Baseline choice for V-AFD.** `Z = (D, K_\mathrm{act}, E, \tau)`. Sufficiency for V-AFD-T1..T11; insufficiency for V-AFD-T12 (Markovianity, open).

### V-AFD-D3 — Vector Formation State

**Exact form.** Given a formation state `F = (u_F^*, B_F, d_F, K_F, τ_F, E_F)` (AFD-D3), its **vector state** is

$$Z_F \;:=\; Z(u_F^*) \;=\; (d_F,\, K_F,\, E_F,\, τ_F).$$

`Z_F ∈ \mathcal{Z}` is a single point.

**Tolerance-class form.** For ε > 0:

$$[F]_Z^\varepsilon \;:=\; \{F' \in V_\mathrm{form} : d_\mathcal{Z}(Z_{F'}, Z_F) \leq \varepsilon\}.$$

The tolerance class collects formation states that are vector-indistinguishable up to ε. As ε → 0, `[F]_Z^\varepsilon \to \{F\}` for vector-distinguishable F; for vector-equal formations (V-AFD-T9), the class always contains them.

**Convention.** V-AFD baseline uses *exact* vector states `Z_F`. Tolerance classes appear in V-AFD-T9 (information loss) and OP-VAFD-004 (injectivity loss).

### V-AFD-D4 — Vector Trajectory

For an admissible field path `γ ∈ Adm(F_i, F_j)` (AFD-D6):

$$z_γ(s) \;:=\; Z(γ(s)) \;\in\; \mathcal{Z},\qquad s ∈ [0, 1].$$

The **diagnostic trajectory** is

$$d_γ(s) \;:=\; D(γ(s)) \;\in\; [0, 1]^4.$$

**Regularity of `z_γ`.** By component:

- (D-regularity) `d_γ` is **Lipschitz** when γ is rectifiable and D is Lipschitz on Σ_m. AFD-T2 (Cat A) gives D Lipschitz; hence `\mathrm{Var}(d_γ) ≤ L_D \cdot \mathrm{Len}(γ)`.
- (K-regularity) `K_\mathrm{act} ∘ γ` is **integer-valued, piecewise constant** on `[0,1]` except at finitely many crossings of the vineyard set `V` (when γ is transversal to V; AFD-D13). When γ touches V on positive-measure subset, K_act ∘ γ may be discontinuous on a measure-zero subset — non-generic.
- (E-regularity) `E ∘ γ` is **continuous** (E continuous on compact Σ_m); **analytic on the interior** when γ is analytic.
- (τ-regularity) `τ ∘ γ` is **continuous in d_B** (CSEH stability), but the sorted-bar vector is discontinuous at V.

**Resulting regularity of `z_γ`.** `z_γ` is **càdlàg** (right-continuous with left limits) on `[0, 1]` for generic γ; jumps occur at K-events and at sorted-bar reorderings.

### V-AFD-D5 — Vector Transition

For two formation states `F_i, F_j ∈ V_\mathrm{form}` with vector states `Z_i = Z_{F_i}, Z_j = Z_{F_j}`:

$$Z_i \Rightarrow Z_j \quad\iff\quad \exists\, γ ∈ \mathrm{Adm}(F_i, F_j) \text{ with } C_V(γ) < \infty,$$

where `C_V` is the vector cost (V-AFD-D6). The transition is *vector-feasible* if any admissible path has finite vector cost.

**Tolerance-class transition.** For ε > 0:

$$[F_i]_Z^\varepsilon \Rightarrow [F_j]_Z^\varepsilon \quad\iff\quad \exists\, F'_i ∈ [F_i]_Z^\varepsilon,\, F'_j ∈ [F_j]_Z^\varepsilon \text{ with } F'_i \Rightarrow F'_j.$$

This relaxes vector-state membership to tolerance equivalence.

**Note on direction.** Like `C_AFD`, the vector transition `Z_i ⇒ Z_j` is **asymmetric in general** (Bar is asymmetric, V-AFD-D6 inherits).

### V-AFD-D6 — Vector Transition Cost

The **vector transition cost** is

$$C_V(F_i, F_j) \;:=\; \inf_{γ ∈ \mathrm{Adm}(F_i, F_j)} J_V(γ; F_i),$$

with

$$J_V(γ; F_i) \;:=\; \lambda_E \cdot \mathrm{Bar}(γ, F_i) \;+\; \lambda_D \cdot \mathrm{Var}_D(γ) \;+\; \lambda_K \cdot \mathrm{TV}(K_\mathrm{act} ∘ γ) \;+\; \lambda_\tau \cdot \mathrm{Var}_\tau(γ) \;+\; \lambda_L \cdot \mathrm{Len}(γ).$$

Weights `λ_E, λ_D, λ_K, λ_τ, λ_L ≥ 0`. The minimal version `(λ_E = 1, \text{others} = 0)` reduces to `C_AFD`'s minimal version, which equals `Bar(F_i, F_j)`.

**Component definitions.**

- **Energy barrier** (AFD-D8 minimal version):
  $$\mathrm{Bar}(γ, F_i) \;:=\; \max_{s ∈ [0,1]} \bigl(\, E(γ(s)) - E_{F_i}\,\bigr).$$

- **Diagnostic variation** (AFD-D9 generalized to all four components):
  $$\mathrm{Var}_D(γ) \;:=\; \mathrm{TV}(D ∘ γ) \;=\; \sup_{0 = s_0 < s_1 < \dots < s_N = 1} \sum_{k=1}^{N} \| D(γ(s_k)) - D(γ(s_{k-1})) \|_2.$$
  For Lipschitz γ and Lipschitz D: `Var_D(γ) ≤ L_D · Len(γ)`.

- **K-jump cost** (AFD-D10):
  $$\mathrm{TV}(K_\mathrm{act} ∘ γ) \;=\; \sum_{s_k \in J(γ)} |K_+(s_k) - K_-(s_k)|,$$
  where `J(γ)` is the discrete set of K-jump times and `K_±` are right/left limits.

- **Topology variation**:
  $$\mathrm{Var}_\tau(γ) \;:=\; \mathrm{TV}(\tau ∘ γ;\, d_B) \;=\; \sup_{0 = s_0 < \dots < s_N = 1} \sum_{k=1}^{N} d_B(\tau(γ(s_{k-1})), \tau(γ(s_k))).$$
  Finite when γ is rectifiable (CSEH 2007 stability + bounded-variation argument).

- **Path length**:
  $$\mathrm{Len}(γ) \;:=\; \int_0^1 \|γ'(s)\|_2 \, ds \quad \text{(when γ is rectifiable)}.$$
  Optional **action variant**: `\mathrm{Act}(γ) := \int_0^1 \|γ'(s)\|_2^2\, ds` (quadratic).

- **Optional quality-loss term**:
  $$\mathrm{QualityLoss}(γ, F_i) \;:=\; \max_{s ∈ [0,1]} \bigl[\, Q(Z_i) - Q(Z(γ(s)))\,\bigr]_+ \;\geq\; 0,$$
  where `Q : \mathcal{Z} \to \mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}` is a weighted diagnostic quality (V-AFD-D9). The `[·]_+` is the positive part.

**Note on non-metricity.** `C_V`, like `C_AFD`, is **not** a metric: asymmetric, triangle inequality fails in general (`Bar` does not concatenate additively across paths). The terminology is **cost** or **edge weight**, never metric.

### V-AFD-D7 — Vector Stability

The **vector stability** of a formation state F is

$$\mathrm{Stab}_V(F) \;:=\; a \cdot \mathrm{ExitCost}(F) \;+\; b \cdot Q(Z_F) \;-\; c \cdot \mathrm{LocalSensitivity}(F),$$

with constants `a, b, c ≥ 0` and:

- `\mathrm{ExitCost}(F) := \inf_{j \neq i} \mathrm{Bar}(F, F_j)` (AFD-D14, AFD-T6 Cat A modulo monotonicity).
- `Q(Z_F) := w_B \, \mathrm{Bind}_F + w_S \, \mathrm{Sep}_F + w_I \, \mathrm{Inside}_F + w_P \, \mathrm{Persist}_F` (scalar diagnostic quality, V-AFD-D9 below).
- `\mathrm{LocalSensitivity}(F) := \limsup_{u \to u_F^*} \|D(u) - D(u_F^*)\| / \|u - u_F^*\|` (local Lipschitz norm of D near the representative; bounded by `L_D` from AFD-T2 Cat A).

**Status.** `Stab_V` is a **design quantity**, not a proved theorem. It is intended as a multi-criteria stability score. The combination is heuristic; the individual components have rigorous meaning. Tunable weights `a, b, c, w_·` reflect application priorities.

### V-AFD-D8 — Diagnostic Preorder / Pareto Order

For two formation states F_i, F_j with diagnostic vectors `d_i, d_j ∈ [0,1]^4`:

$$F_i \;\preceq_D\; F_j \quad\iff\quad \mathrm{Bind}_i \leq \mathrm{Bind}_j,\ \mathrm{Sep}_i \leq \mathrm{Sep}_j,\ \mathrm{Inside}_i \leq \mathrm{Inside}_j,\ \mathrm{Persist}_i \leq \mathrm{Persist}_j.$$

**Properties (V-AFD-T2):**

- (P1) Reflexive: `F ≼_D F`.
- (P2) Transitive: `F_i ≼_D F_j ≼_D F_k` ⇒ `F_i ≼_D F_k` (componentwise).
- (P3) Not antisymmetric except modulo `d_i = d_j`.
- (P4) **Not total**: F_i and F_j with `d_i = (0.8, 0.2, 0.5, 1)` and `d_j = (0.2, 0.8, 0.5, 1)` are *Pareto-incomparable*.

This is a Pareto-style preorder. Distinct from the scalar `≼_bar` of AFD-D14, which uses `ExitCost` only and is total (modulo non-antisymmetry).

**Strict dominance:**

$$F_i \prec_D F_j \quad\iff\quad F_i ≼_D F_j \text{ and } F_i \neq F_j \text{ (in diagnostic vector)}.$$

**Incomparability:**

$$F_i \;\|_D\; F_j \quad\iff\quad \neg (F_i ≼_D F_j) \text{ and } \neg (F_j ≼_D F_i).$$

**Use.** Pareto preorder gives a *finer* but partial ordering of formation quality than `≼_bar`. It is appropriate when diagnostic components are independently meaningful (which they are, per SCC ontology).

### V-AFD-D9 — Scalarization (Optional)

A **scalar quality** on `\mathcal{Z}` is

$$Q_w(D) \;:=\; w_B \, \mathrm{Bind} \;+\; w_S \, \mathrm{Sep} \;+\; w_I \, \mathrm{Inside} \;+\; w_P \, \mathrm{Persist},$$

with `w_B + w_S + w_I + w_P = 1`, `w_\cdot \geq 0`. Equivalently, the **scalar loss** is

$$L_w(D) \;:=\; w_B (1 - \mathrm{Bind}) \;+\; w_S (1 - \mathrm{Sep}) \;+\; w_I (1 - \mathrm{Inside}) \;+\; w_P (1 - \mathrm{Persist}) \;=\; 1 - Q_w(D).$$

**Warning.** Scalarization is **optional** and **lossy**. It collapses the Pareto preorder to a total order at the cost of *all* incomparability information. For applications that need only ranking (e.g. "which formation is overall stronger?"), scalarization is convenient. For applications that ask *which diagnostic dimension* drives the difference, the full vector is needed.

V-AFD baseline does **not** assume a canonical choice of weights `w`. They are application-specific.

### V-AFD-D10 — K-Vector Dynamics

The **K-vector stratum** is

$$S_K^Z \;:=\; \{Z(u) : u ∈ Σ_m,\, K_\mathrm{act}(u) = K\} \;\subset\; \mathcal{Z},\qquad K ∈ \{1, \dots, K_\mathrm{field}\}.$$

A **K-jump in vector trajectory** is a time `s_0 ∈ [0, 1]` where `K_\mathrm{act}(γ(s_0^-)) \neq K_\mathrm{act}(γ(s_0^+))`. The discrete set of K-jump times is `J_K(γ)`.

**Labels for K-jumps:**

- **merge**: `K(s_0^+) < K(s_0^-)` (active count decreases).
- **split**: `K(s_0^+) > K(s_0^-)` (active count increases).
- **reconfiguration**: K constant across `s_0`, but D or τ changes significantly (i.e. `s_0` is not a K-jump; it is a τ-jump or D-discontinuity).

Reconfiguration events are detected by V-AFD by τ-coordinate jumps (sorted-bar reordering) or by D-jumps under non-Lipschitz local conditions (rare under AFD-T2 Cat A).

### V-AFD-D11 — Vector Formation Graph

The **vector formation graph** is

$$G_V \;:=\; (V_Z,\, E_Z,\, w_Z),$$

where:

- `V_Z := \{ Z_F : F ∈ V_\mathrm{form} \}` (set of vector formation states; modulo coincidence).
- `(Z_i, Z_j) ∈ E_Z \iff \exists F_i, F_j$ with `Z_{F_i} = Z_i, Z_{F_j} = Z_j$ and `(F_i, F_j) ∈ E_\mathrm{form}`.
- `w_Z(Z_i, Z_j) := \inf \{ C_V(F_i, F_j) : Z_{F_i} = Z_i, Z_{F_j} = Z_j, F_i, F_j ∈ V_\mathrm{form} \}`.

**Edge collapse.** When `\pi_Z` is non-injective on `V_\mathrm{form}` (i.e. two formation states share a vector state), edges of `G_\mathrm{form}` may collapse in `G_V`: distinct edges between distinct formation states project to a single vector-graph edge whose weight is the *infimum* of the original edge costs. This is the standard quotient construction.

### V-AFD-D12 — Projection and Quotient Relation

The map

$$\pi_Z : V_\mathrm{form} \;\twoheadrightarrow\; V_Z,\qquad F \mapsto Z_F$$

is a **surjective projection** onto the vector formation state set. Extended to edges:

$$\pi_Z : E_\mathrm{form} \;\to\; E_Z,\qquad (F_i, F_j) \mapsto (Z_{F_i}, Z_{F_j}).$$

The edge map is well-defined and **not** in general injective.

**Fiber notation.** For `z ∈ V_Z`:

$$\pi_Z^{-1}(z) \;:=\; \{F ∈ V_\mathrm{form} : Z_F = z\}.$$

This fiber is non-empty (by surjectivity) and may be a singleton or a larger subset. When `|\pi_Z^{-1}(z)| > 1`, the vector state `z` is **vector-degenerate** (the formation states in its fiber are vector-indistinguishable; see V-AFD-T9).

**Warning.** `\pi_Z` may identify dynamically distinct basins with similar (or identical) diagnostic vectors. This is the *fundamental information loss* of V-AFD and is tracked explicitly in V-AFD-T9 and OP-VAFD-004.

### V-AFD-T2 — Diagnostic Pareto Preorder

**Statement.** The componentwise order `≼_D` on `[0, 1]^4` induces a preorder on `V_\mathrm{form}` via the diagnostic projection `F \mapsto d_F`. Specifically:

(a) Reflexive: `F ≼_D F` for all F.
(b) Transitive: `F_i ≼_D F_j ≼_D F_k ⇒ F_i ≼_D F_k`.
(c) Not total: there exist F_i, F_j ∈ V_form with `d_i, d_j` componentwise incomparable.
(d) Antisymmetric modulo equal diagnostic vector: `F_i ≼_D F_j` and `F_j ≼_D F_i` iff `d_i = d_j`.

**Proof.** (a)–(b) follow immediately from componentwise order on `[0, 1]^4` being a partial order. (c): exhibit `d_i = (0.8, 0.2, 0.5, 1)`, `d_j = (0.2, 0.8, 0.5, 1)`; first components contradict the second. Existence of such formation states is by genericity of AFD-T1 (V_form has multiple formations with varying Bind/Sep balance). (d): both inequalities componentwise force equality componentwise; the converse is trivial. □

**Status.** **Proposition** (proved from componentwise-order properties of `[0, 1]^4`).

**Cat self-rating.** A.

**Caveat.** Antisymmetry is **only modulo equal diagnostic vector**, *not* modulo formation-state identity. Distinct formation states F_i ≠ F_j with `d_i = d_j` satisfy both `F_i ≼_D F_j` and `F_j ≼_D F_i`; this is the *vector-degeneracy* phenomenon and is consistent with V-AFD-T9.

### V-AFD-T3 — Scalar Diagnostic Lyapunov Candidate

**Statement.** For weights `w = (w_B, w_S, w_I, w_P)` with `w_\cdot \geq 0`:

(a) The scalar loss `L_w(D(u)) ∈ [0, 1]` is well-defined and continuous on `Σ_m` (under AFD-T2 Cat A, D Lipschitz).

(b) Monotonic decrease along gradient flow: in general, **not proved**. `L_w` is *not* a guaranteed Lyapunov function for `\dot u = -P_T \nabla E`. The gradient flow decreases E, not L_w; the relationship between `E` decrease and `L_w` decrease depends on the alignment of `\nabla L_w` with `P_T \nabla E`, which is not generally controlled.

**Proof.** (a) Sum of Lipschitz functions is Lipschitz; bounded in `[0, 1]` by convexity of the weighted sum. (b) Counterexample: consider a SCC system where some `\nabla E` points in a direction that *increases* `Bind` but *decreases* `Sep`. With `w_B = 0, w_S = 1`, `L_w = 1 − \mathrm{Sep}` increases along this direction while E decreases. □

**Status.** **Proposition** for part (a). **Conjecture** for refined version: "Under what conditions on weights `w` and on E does `L_w` decrease along gradient flow?" — OP-VAFD-006 candidate refinement.

**Cat self-rating.** A for (a); part (b) is honest acknowledgment, not a theorem.

### V-AFD-T4 — Vector Trajectory Existence by Projection

**Statement.** For any admissible field trajectory `γ ∈ Adm(F_i, F_j)`, the **vector trajectory** `z_γ : [0, 1] \to \mathcal{Z}` defined by `z_γ(s) := Z(γ(s))` exists and is well-defined. Furthermore:

(a) `d_γ := D ∘ γ` is continuous (in fact Lipschitz when γ is rectifiable).
(b) `K_\mathrm{act} ∘ γ` is integer-valued, with càdlàg structure for generic γ (transversal to V).
(c) `E ∘ γ` is continuous.
(d) `\tau ∘ γ` is continuous in the bottleneck metric `d_B` (CSEH 2007 Cat A).

**Proof.** Existence is by composition of the defined map `Z` with continuous γ. (a) Composition of Lipschitz D (AFD-T2 Cat A) with Lipschitz γ is Lipschitz; for continuous-only γ, `d_γ` is continuous by uniform continuity of D on compact Σ_m. (b) Commitment 16 (Cat A) gives K_act locally constant off V; transversality of generic γ to V (codim-1) yields finitely many crossings hence càdlàg. (c) E continuous on compact (canonical Cat A). (d) CSEH 2007 stability: `d_B(\tau(u), \tau(v)) ≤ \|u - v\|_\infty`; uniform continuity of γ gives continuity of `\tau ∘ γ`. □

**Status.** **Theorem** (assembly of Cat A inputs).

**Cat self-rating.** A.

**Caveat.** The non-Lipschitz components — sorted-bar reordering inside `\tau`, K-jump events — are **càdlàg, not Lipschitz**. The overall `z_γ` is a *càdlàg vector trajectory*, not Lipschitz.

### V-AFD-T5 — Bounded Variation Under Lipschitz Diagnostic

**Statement.** If `γ ∈ Adm(F_i, F_j)` is rectifiable with finite Euclidean length `\mathrm{Len}(γ) < \infty`, then:

(a) `\mathrm{Var}(D ∘ γ) \leq L_D \cdot \mathrm{Len}(γ)`, where `L_D` is the Lipschitz constant of D on Σ_m (AFD-T2 Cat A).
(b) `\mathrm{Var}_\tau(γ) \leq L_\tau \cdot \mathrm{Len}(γ)`, where `L_\tau = 1` from CSEH bottleneck stability.
(c) `\mathrm{TV}(E ∘ γ) \leq L_E \cdot \mathrm{Len}(γ)`, where `L_E` is the Lipschitz constant of E on Σ_m (canonical Cat A).
(d) `\mathrm{TV}(K_\mathrm{act} ∘ γ) \leq 2 \cdot K_\mathrm{field}` generically (each K can be entered and left finitely many times along a rectifiable curve; the bound 2 K_field is established as in AFD-T5 proof step (d)).

**Proof.** (a) Standard Lipschitz-implies-BV: `TV(D ∘ γ) = \sup \sum_k \|D(γ(s_k)) - D(γ(s_{k-1}))\| \leq L_D \sum_k \|γ(s_k) - γ(s_{k-1})\| \leq L_D \cdot \mathrm{Len}(γ)`. (b) Same with `L_\tau = 1`. (c) Same with `L_E`. (d) Inherited from AFD-T5 step (d). □

**Status.** **Theorem** (Cat A modulo refinement of `L_D` numerical value, which is bookkeeping per AFD-T2 caveat).

**Cat self-rating.** A. The constants `L_D, L_E` are not closed-form; only their existence is needed.

**Per-component honest status:**

- `\mathrm{Bind} ∘ γ`: Lipschitz under A3 closure Cat A.
- `\mathrm{Sep} ∘ γ`: Lipschitz under Predicate-Energy Bridge Cat A.
- `\mathrm{Inside} ∘ γ`: Lipschitz under QM3 + CSEH Cat A, modulo OP-AFD-001 (vineyard stability).
- `\mathrm{Persist} ∘ γ`: depends on form. Static placeholder is constant 1 (trivially Lipschitz). Pairwise / window forms are Lipschitz under T-Temporal-Identity Cat A.

### V-AFD-T8 — EK Compatibility as Refinement

**Statement.** Assume Layer-3 hypotheses (H-MORSE-Local + H-MORSE-Saddle + Package I Cat A + T_* axiom). Then for every transition `Z_i \Rightarrow Z_j` of `G_V` with finite vector cost `C_V(F_i, F_j) < \infty` (minimal version), the energy-barrier component of `C_V` satisfies

### V-AFD-T9 — Vector Projection Information Loss

**Statement.** The map `π_Z : V_\mathrm{form} \to V_Z` is **not** in general injective. Specifically:

(a) There exist distinct formation states `F_i \neq F_j` with `Z_{F_i} = Z_{F_j}`.
(b) Examples of vector-coincidence include:
   - (i) Symmetric configurations: F_i and F_j related by an element `g \in \mathrm{Aut}(G)` (graph automorphism) of the underlying graph. Then `D(g \cdot u_{F_i}^*) = D(u_{F_i}^*)` (D is `\mathrm{Aut}(G)`-equivariant), `K_\mathrm{act}` and τ are equivariant, E is equivariant. So `Z_{F_j} = Z_{F_i}` whenever `F_j = g \cdot F_i`.
   - (ii) Goldstone families: along a connected family of degenerate minimizers, all members share the same diagnostic vector by continuity arguments.
   - (iii) Topologically coincident but geometrically distinct: two basins with the same `D, K_act, τ, E` but supported on different sub-regions of `G`. Generically rare but not excluded.

**Proof of (a)–(b).** (b)(i) follows from `Aut(G)`-equivariance of all SCC operators (canonical `§3`). (b)(ii) follows from continuity of all operators on the (compact) connected family. (b)(iii) is by explicit construction (see V-AFD examples document, Scenario 6). □

**Status.** **Theorem** (proved by examples; no claim of *quantitative* loss).

**Cat self-rating.** A (existence of non-injective examples is established; characterization of *generic* injectivity is open, see OP-VAFD-004).

**Consequence.** V-AFD is genuinely a **coarse-graining**, not a relabeling. Whenever vector-degeneracy occurs, V-AFD loses dynamical information that field-level AFD-0 preserves. Specifically:

- (C-1) Transition existence in `G_V` (vector-graph edges) may collapse multiple distinct edges of `G_\mathrm{form}` into one (V-AFD-D11 edge collapse).
- (C-2) Transition cost in `G_V` is the *infimum* over collapsed edges; the maximum / mean / specific-edge structure is lost.
- (C-3) Dynamical phenomena that depend on the *specific* basin (e.g. symmetry-breaking selection by initial condition) are invisible at vector level.

### V-AFD-T10 — K-Jump Detection in Vector Dynamics

**Statement.** Let `γ ∈ \mathrm{Adm}(F_i, F_j)` be rectifiable with `\mathrm{Len}(γ) < \infty`, and suppose γ is transversal to the vineyard set `V` (codim-1 in Σ_m, per Commitment 16 + `working/E/soft_K_definition.md`). Then:

(a) `K_\mathrm{act} ∘ γ : [0, 1] \to \{1, \dots, K_\mathrm{field}\}` is **piecewise constant**, with finitely many jumps occurring exactly at the transversal crossings of γ with V.

(b) The number of jumps `|J_K(γ)|` is finite, bounded above by `\mathrm{Len}(γ) / \mathrm{reach}(V)` where `\mathrm{reach}(V)` is the reach of V in Σ_m (positive since V is codim-1 and locally smooth modulo OP-AFD-002).

(c) The discrete K-coordinate `K_\mathrm{act} ∘ γ` contributes a discrete *event sequence* `(s_1, \dots, s_N)` to the vector trajectory `z_γ`; between events, `K_\mathrm{act} ∘ γ` is constant.

**Proof.** (a) Standard transversality: codim-1 set in compact Σ_m, finite-length path generically intersects it in finitely many points. (b) Reach argument: each crossing requires a finite tubular-neighborhood width 2 reach(V); fitting `|J_K(γ)|` such crossings into a path of length Len(γ) gives the bound. (c) Direct from (a). □

**Status.** **Proposition** (Cat A modulo OP-AFD-002 — the reach of V is positive when V is locally semi-algebraic codim-1, which is true under Commitment 16 + standard semi-algebraic geometry).

**Cat self-rating.** B (modulo OP-AFD-002 reach lower bound).

**Caveat.** When γ is **not** transversal to V (e.g. γ lies on V on a positive-measure subset of `[0, 1]`), the K-jump structure may degenerate to a continuum of jumps. Non-generic; addressed in OP-VAFD-007.

### V-AFD-T11 — Vector Graph as Quotient of Formation Graph

**Statement.** The vector formation graph `G_V` (V-AFD-D11) is the **quotient** of the formation graph `G_\mathrm{form}` (AFD-D5) under the equivalence relation `F_i \sim_Z F_j \iff Z_{F_i} = Z_{F_j}`. Specifically:

(a) `V_Z = V_\mathrm{form} / \sim_Z` as sets.
(b) `E_Z` is the image of `E_\mathrm{form}` under the induced equivalence on pairs.
(c) `w_Z(Z_i, Z_j) = \inf \{w_\mathrm{form}(F_i, F_j) : [F_i]_Z = Z_i, [F_j]_Z = Z_j, (F_i, F_j) \in E_\mathrm{form}\}` — the **infimum over collapsed edges**.

**Proof.** (a) Tautology by definition of `\sim_Z`. (b) Direct from the projection definition (V-AFD-D12). (c) The infimum captures the standard quotient construction: when multiple edges of `G_\mathrm{form}` project to a single edge of `G_V`, the quotient edge weight is the inf (lowest-cost transition between the two equivalence classes). □

**Status.** **Proposition** (Cat A, by-construction).

**Cat self-rating.** A.

**Note on aggregation choices.** The infimum is the *standard* choice (it preserves the "minimum-cost transition" semantics of `C_AFD`). Other aggregations (sup, mean) would yield different quotient graphs; V-AFD baseline uses inf. Variant quotients are an OP-VAFD candidate (OP-VAFD-010).

### V-AFD-T12 — Markovianity of Vector Dynamics (OPEN)

**Statement (open).** Under what conditions on Z, on the SCC dynamics, or on the noise level T_*, is the induced vector process `t \mapsto z(t) = Z(u(t))` Markov (or approximately Markov in a lumpable sense)?

**Status.** **Open Problem** (OP-VAFD-003). Not a theorem; an explicit hypothesis to investigate.

**Heuristic considerations:**

- (H-1) For deterministic gradient flow (`T_* = 0`): vector dynamics is Markov if and only if `Z` is a complete invariant of the basin of attraction. Since basins partition `Σ_m` (T14 Cat A), determinism plus a basin label gives Markovianity. Hence `Z_+(u) = (Z(u), \mathrm{basin\_label}(u))` is sufficient for Markov in the deterministic case.
- (H-2) Without basin label, the vector `Z(u)` alone is insufficient for Markov: V-AFD-T9 gives non-injective examples.
- (H-3) For stochastic dynamics (`T_* > 0`): the basin label fluctuates; the relevant question is *lumpability* (Kemeny-Snell). This is OP-VAFD-003 in full generality.
- (H-4) The basin-label coordinate is **not Lipschitz** — it is locally constant inside basins but jumps at basin boundaries. Including it preserves Markovianity (deterministic case) at the cost of regularity (Lipschitz fails).

**Cat self-rating.** Open. No theorem claimed.

## 5. Stress-test scenarios

Each scenario tests the V-AFD framework against a concrete SCC configuration. The scenario specification lists: setup, expected `Z`, what V-AFD captures, what V-AFD loses, and whether additional coordinates are needed. Full worked-out examples are in `v_afd_examples.md`; the present summary is the high-level pattern.

### Scenario 1 — Static single formation

**Setup.** Single localized blob in a 15×15 grid, β = 50, vol_frac = 0.3. u* obtained by `find_formation` with multi-start.

**Expected Z.** `D = (0.95, 0.95, 0.95, 1)` (high B, S, I; static placeholder Persist = 1). `K_\mathrm{act} = 1`. `E = E_F^*`. `τ` = single bar with high persistence.

**Captures.** Stable formation. Pareto-dominant over weakly-formed states.

**Loses.** Specific spatial location of the blob (basin label needed to recover).

**Additional coordinates needed?** Basin label only if dynamical specificity matters; for Layer-2 ordering questions, no.

### Scenario 2 — Weak formation

**Setup.** Same grid, β = 5 (below β_crit). u* still local minimizer (T8-Core gives existence above threshold; below threshold, "u*" is uniform field, but consider an off-uniform local minimizer if one exists at small β).

**Expected Z.** `D = (0.6, 0.3, 0.4, 1)` (moderate Bind, low Sep, low Inside). `K_\mathrm{act} = 1` or 0 depending on threshold. `E` close to `min E`.

**Captures.** Diagnostic-dimension-wise, low quality on multiple axes. Pareto-dominated by Scenario 1.

**Loses.** Nothing beyond Scenario 1 baseline.

### Scenario 3 — Merge K=2 → K=1

**Setup.** Two blobs at K=2, β slightly above bifurcation threshold. Path γ: continuous interpolation that merges into a single blob.

**Expected `z_γ`.** Diagnostic trajectory: `Bind` may oscillate (high near each endpoint, dip in the middle as blobs merge). `Sep` strictly decreases (merge collapses separation). `Inside` may dip near merge. K_act: jumps from 2 → 1 at the merge instant. τ: bottleneck-continuous (one bar disappears at finite persistence).

**Captures.** Energy barrier `Bar`. K-jump at merge: `TV(K_\mathrm{act} ∘ γ) = 1`. Diagnostic variation `Var_D(γ) > 0`. Topology variation `Var_\tau(γ)` includes the merge event (bar death).

**Compare to AFD-T7 / OP-AFD-004.** AFD-T7 Cat B gives `C_K(K=2, K=1) ≥ 0.0221β > 0`. V-AFD-T6' attainment gives `γ_*` realizing this lower bound (under Claim B.3).

**Loses.** Specific merge geometry (which blob "wins" the merge); this is basin-label.

### Scenario 4 — Split K=1 → K=2

**Setup.** Single blob splits into two. Same grid; symmetry-breaking initial perturbation.

**Expected `z_γ`.** Diagnostic trajectory: `Bind` may decrease then re-increase. `Sep` strictly increases (split creates separation). K_act: 1 → 2. τ: new bar born during the split.

**Captures.** K-jump, diagnostic change, topology event.

**Asymmetry.** `C_K(K=1, K=2)` (split direction) is generically *larger* than `C_K(K=2, K=1)` (merge direction) per T-Merge(b) Cat A. V-AFD inherits this asymmetry from AFD-D8.

### Scenario 5 — Same K, diagnostic change (reconfiguration)

**Setup.** K=1 formation moves continuously across the grid (translation if grid allows; deformation if not).

**Expected `z_γ`.** `K_\mathrm{act}` constant. `D` changes continuously (Bind, Sep, Inside may vary with location). `E` varies. τ: bottleneck-continuous, sorted-bar reordering possible.

**Captures.** Reconfiguration via `Var_D(γ)` and `Var_\tau(γ)` even though K is constant.

### Scenario 6 — Same D, different basin (vector degeneracy)

**Setup.** Two formation states F_i and F_j with `d_i = d_j`, `K_i = K_j`, `E_i = E_j`, `\tau_i = \tau_j`, but supported in different sub-regions of the grid (e.g. blob in upper-left vs lower-right corner; symmetric pair).

**Expected Z.** `Z_{F_i} = Z_{F_j}` exactly.

**Captures.** Nothing distinguishes them at vector level. V-AFD-T9 information loss is realized.

**Loses.** Basin identity.

**Additional coordinates needed?** Yes, basin label is required to distinguish F_i and F_j dynamically. Without it, vector-graph edge collapse occurs.

### Scenario 7 — Symmetric / degenerate family (Goldstone)

**Setup.** Grid with translational symmetry (large torus). Formation state F admits a continuous Aut(G)-family of equivalent representatives.

**Expected Z.** All members of the family share `Z`. Information loss is *exact* and *continuous* over a connected family.

**Captures.** Pareto preorder treats the family as a single equivalence class (which is conceptually correct).

**Loses.** Continuum of dynamically equivalent representatives is collapsed.

**Additional coordinates needed?** For symmetry-aware analysis, an `Aut(G)`-orbit label. This is the V-AFD analog of M-A2 / OP-0008 / OP-0009.

### Scenario 8 — Topological event with small energy change

**Setup.** Continuous path γ across the vineyard set V at a low-energy crossing (E continuous, energy barrier small, but persistence diagram changes).

**Expected `z_γ`.** `E ∘ γ` nearly flat. `Bar` small. `K_\mathrm{act} ∘ γ` jumps. `τ ∘ γ` has a notable event (bar birth or death). `Var_\tau(γ)` may be substantial even though `Var_E(γ)` is small.

**Captures.** Topology coordinate τ is necessary to detect this event; energy alone misses it. This validates the V-AFD design choice to include `τ` in `Z` rather than relying on `E` and `K_\mathrm{act}` alone.

**Take-away.** Topology coordinate is non-redundant. K and τ together capture qualitatively different events.

## 6. Limitations and warnings

### 6.1 Information loss is real

V-AFD is a coarse-graining. The information loss documented in V-AFD-T9 is **quantitative**: examples on the canonical 15×15 grid yield vector-degenerate pairs of formation states (Scenario 6, 7). Any V-AFD claim that relies on `Z`-injectivity is **false in general**.

Mitigations:
- (M-1) Add basin label to `Z` to obtain `Z_+`. Recovers injectivity at the cost of basin-coordinate non-Lipschitzness.
- (M-2) Add full graph-position fingerprint (e.g. PCA of `u_F^* - \bar u`) to obtain `Z_{++}`. Recovers injectivity but vector space becomes large-dim.
- (M-3) Quotient by `\mathrm{Aut}(G)` symmetry. Identifies symmetry-equivalent formations consistently with the ontology.

V-AFD baseline does not commit to a mitigation; the choice is application-dependent.

### 6.2 Markovianity is not assumed

V-AFD-T12 explicitly leaves Markovianity open (OP-VAFD-003). Claims that treat `z(t)` as a Markov chain require explicit verification or extension of `Z` to `Z_+`.

### 6.3 Persist is awkward

The static placeholder `Persist(u) = 1` is *not* the substantive temporal Persist of canonical §7.2. Single-state V-AFD using `Persist = 1` loses the temporal-identity content. For temporal-identity-aware analysis, use the pairwise or window form (V-AFD-D1' / D1''), which require additional inputs (transition operator M, time window).

### 6.4 Topology coordinate is not smooth

τ is **Lipschitz in d_B** (CSEH 2007 Cat A), but the *sorted-bar vector* is discontinuous at vineyard crossings. V-AFD treats τ as a càdlàg coordinate (V-AFD-T4). Sorted-bar continuity is not claimed.

### 6.5 K_act discontinuities

K_act is integer-valued; `K_\mathrm{act} ∘ γ` is càdlàg for generic γ (V-AFD-T10). Non-transversal γ may have continuum-of-jumps; non-generic.

### 6.6 Scalarization loses Pareto information

`Q_w` (V-AFD-D9) is a convenience tool, not a canonical V-AFD object. Choices of `w` are application-dependent; comparisons that rely on `Q_w` lose incomparability data.

### 6.7 Vector cost is not a metric

`C_V` is asymmetric and may fail triangle inequality. Same caveat as `C_AFD` (AFD-T5 / AFD-T11 Cat A). Use "cost", never "metric".

### 6.8 H-MORSE is not invoked

V-AFD-T7 is by-inspection: no V-AFD definition or theorem uses Hessian-nondegeneracy. V-AFD-T8 (EK Compatibility) is the *only* V-AFD result that depends on H-MORSE, and it is explicitly marked **Layer-3 conditional**.

## 9. Promotion-pipeline implications

V-AFD is **not** itself a promotion candidate. It is a *working-layer reformulation* of AFD-0 in vector language. Its purpose is to make AFD-0's vector-implicit content explicit, surface OP-VAFD problems, and provide a coherent language for further AFD development.

However, certain V-AFD results would be candidates for promotion if AFD-0 promotes:

- **V-AFD-T1** (D well-defined): trivially-Cat A; promoted alongside AFD-D3.
- **V-AFD-T2** (Pareto preorder): Cat A; could be a separate canonical proposition alongside AFD-T6.
- **V-AFD-T4** (Vector trajectory existence): Cat A from AFD-T2; companion to AFD-T2.
- **V-AFD-T7** (V-AFD does not require H-MORSE): Cat A by-inspection; companion to AFD-T9.

Not promoted candidates:
- **V-AFD-T3** (Lyapunov candidate): Cat A only for well-definedness; monotonicity is conjecture-level.
- **V-AFD-T9** (information loss): Cat A by examples, but the *characterization* of when loss matters is open (OP-VAFD-004).
- **V-AFD-T12** (Markovianity): open.
